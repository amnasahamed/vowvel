# Conservatory opening film

The website already works with CSS envelope animation. This optional cinematic film can replace the visual opening later without gating guest details.

## Ready-to-use frames

- First: assets/video-frames/conservatory-first.png
- Last: assets/video-frames/conservatory-last.png

Both are 1672 × 941 landscape frames. Generated with built-in image generation. They intentionally contain no couple names so editable text can remain in HTML. Minor material differences should be checked after interpolation.

## Video prompt

Locked overhead camera. Keep the tabletop, flowers, ribbon and sage envelope completely stationary. Over 3.5 seconds, the champagne wax seal gently releases and settles below the envelope; the triangular paper flap unfolds upward with realistic paper stiffness; the illustrated ivory card slowly glides upward to match the last frame. Tactile shadows change naturally. No hands, added objects, new text, warping, scene transitions or camera movement. Delicate, luxurious and physically plausible. Ease gently into a completely still final half second. No audio.

Export a 24 fps H.264 MP4 and, if available, a WebM version. Aim for under 2 MB. Keep a still poster. Playback should start only after the guest taps the seal. Keep Skip visible; reduced-motion users get the final still immediately. Do not burn names into the film or autoplay sound. Mobile needs a separately composed portrait version rather than a center crop that cuts off paper.

A single beautifully made CLOSED sage green cotton-paper envelope centered on warm ivory handmade paper tabletop, flap facing camera, small antique champagne wax seal with a simple abstract V incision centered where flap meets. Envelope occupies central 55 percent of image width, landscape rectangle, straight almost horizontal only rotated minus 5 degrees. Delicate pressed white cosmos and fine olive branches at far upper left and lower right corners, loose soft ivory silk ribbon at far right edge. Natural diffused late-afternoon window light from top left, subtle shadows, exquisitely tactile and real, high-end stationery product photography, restrained luxury, enough plain ivory negative space around envelope for crop safety. Envelope closed, no invitation card visible. No text anywhere except abstract tiny V seal, no hands no people no watermark no frame.
