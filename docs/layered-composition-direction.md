# Vowvel: compose materials before scenes

The first implementation of this approach is the Conservatory guest invitation. The approved storefront and the four other guest directions have not been redesigned in this pass.

## Composition order

1. Fine woven background: a quiet surface, not a scenic wallpaper.
2. Sage backing paper: slightly rotated and casting a physical shadow.
3. Separate botanical spray tucked behind the paper.
4. Torn cotton-paper PNG: real transparent irregular edges and visible fibers.
5. Live HTML typography: names, date and venue remain editable and accessible.
6. Foreground botanical cutout: overlaps the lower paper edge to establish depth.
7. Translucent vellum band: reveals the materials underneath.
8. Separate silk ribbon PNG: sits above the vellum.

Each object is its own DOM layer. Paper enters, type settles and flower stems sway independently; reduced-motion disables the animations. The flowers reappear as a small story-photo embellishment and closing motif. The scratch and reply cards use the torn paper asset.

## Rule for subsequent directions

Design the composition and occlusion order first. Generate the individual parts second. Give movement a physical reason. Use a whole background scene only when the scene itself is required—not as a replacement for composing an invitation.
