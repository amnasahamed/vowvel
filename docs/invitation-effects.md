# Invitation effects

Opening covers and videos are unchanged. Effects attach only to the invitation content after the opener completes. Existing art is reused; decorative flowers and botanical lines are inline vectors.

- Conservatory: floating hero flowers, drawn botanical welcome border, closing fireflies.
- Gulmohar: falling hero petals, warm shimmer on existing monograms.
- After Hours: hero sparkle, candlelight in the programme, swaying fabric edges beside the story.
- Sunday Edit: names settling with gravity, a tear-tab welcome note, swipe/arrow photo stack, confetti after a successful attending RSVP (including the explicitly labelled demo reply).
- Azure: subtle hero parallax, reversible welcome postcard, ripple after revealing the surprise, wave-shaped reveal of the practical notes.

Decorative effects are pointer-transparent and hidden from assistive technology. Section observers pause ambient decoration offscreen; tab visibility pauses animation. Reduced motion disables decorative movement and reveal transitions. Embedded editor previews omit atmospheric motion. Interactive note and postcard controls remain usable with a keyboard and announce expanded state. The photo stack supports touch, buttons and arrow keys.

Verification: production build and 39 existing tests passed. Browser checks covered all five templates at 390×844 and 1280×720, absence of horizontal overflow, note reveal, postcard flip, photo navigation, preview RSVP confetti, date ripple, and unchanged mobile opener selection. Hero layout sizing remains bounded to each hero.
