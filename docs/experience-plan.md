# Vowvel — five invitation worlds

Build plan · 15 September 2026 · Wedding and engagement only

## Product idea

An invitation should feel like receiving a small work of art. The craft earns the opening; clear information earns the RSVP. Show the envelope interaction before asking for an account. Let a couple put their names into any design and experience the result before paying.

The five designs must differ in **materials, composition, typography, reveal mechanism, and section rhythm**. A shared content model can power distinct art direction. Changing the palette alone does not create another design.

Use ₹5,000 as an internal perceived-quality target, not a fabricated comparison price. Final retail price, taxes, hosting duration, and refund terms remain business decisions. Until approved, checkout is explicitly a demonstration and cannot collect money.

## 1. Azure — a letter from the coast

- **World:** white cotton paper, Mediterranean cobalt watercolor, pale sand, embossed tide marks. Intimate coastal stationery craftsmanship with inclusive ceremony wording.
- **Composition:** asymmetrical editorial letter; oversized names broken across two lines; generous margins; small letterpress captions; torn-edge photo tipped into the page.
- **Opening:** an addressed envelope rests on the table. Tap the wax seal; it releases with a restrained 120 ms compression. The flap lifts, the letter slides out, then resolves into the live page in about 1.3 seconds.
- **Sections:** personal opening letter → couple portrait/contact sheet → date keepsake → ceremony cards laid like stationery inserts → venue postcard → RSVP reply slip → handwritten closing.
- **Scratch:** rub a small foil postage stamp to reveal a personal note: “Our favourite day needs our favourite people.” Date remains readable outside the stamp.
- **Motion:** card edges lift on hover, one letter emerges on open, photographs settle a few degrees. No continuous parallax.
- **Asset:** overhead ivory stationery still life, cotton fibres, blue watercolor coastal vignette, cobalt wax seal. No baked-in names.

## 2. The Conservatory — a garden pressed between pages

- **World:** hand-painted white blossoms, sage foliage, warm ivory paper, translucent vellum; joyful botanical craft without stock floral borders.
- **Composition:** specimen-book spreads; irregular illustration placement; fine handwritten annotations paired with a composed serif; illustrations overlap edges while text stays clear.
- **Opening:** a translucent botanical envelope with a paper belly band. Tap to slide the band away; two vellum leaves part and a flower illustration unfolds into the invitation margin.
- **Sections:** garden title spread → “How we grew together” story chapters → pressed-flower date card → ceremony herbarium labels → illustrated venue itinerary → dress palette swatches → RSVP seed-paper card.
- **Scratch:** brush away a delicate pollen-colored coating to uncover the couple's chosen flower and a personal memory. Accessible reveal button alongside.
- **Motion:** three restrained leaf layers, petal entrance once, gentle paper slide. Artwork never obscures directions or controls.
- **Asset:** original gouache botanical compositions on warm ivory, strong negative space, isolated blossoms for reusable corners.

## 3. Gulmohar — a miniature world of celebration

- **World:** Indian miniature-painting geometry, vermilion, aged rose, restrained brass, hand-blocked borders. Architecture and craft carry grandeur; no generic palace photograph or religious assumptions.
- **Composition:** a ceremonial folio with arched framed panels; rich opening followed by calm parchment information sections; architectural cutouts and precise repeated borders.
- **Opening:** a gatefold invitation with a silk tie. Tap the central medallion; tie releases and the illustrated paper doors open laterally, revealing a layered courtyard.
- **Sections:** illuminated names → welcome beneath an arch → constellation date tile → procession-like ceremony timeline → venue courtyard map card → attire and travel illustrated inserts → RSVP brass-edged reply card.
- **Scratch:** polish a small moon medallion to reveal a host-selected blessing or welcome line; never require a particular religion or language.
- **Motion:** gate leaves rotate in perspective, four scenery layers separate subtly, border motifs draw once. Reduced motion swaps directly to the folio.
- **Asset:** handcrafted miniature courtyard with roses and cypress, gouache texture, no real venue misrepresentation, no text.

## 4. After Hours — the celebration after dark

- **World:** black cherry, warm brass, art-deco geometry, analog grain. Contemporary, intimate, cinematic supper-club glamour; works particularly well with real couple portraits.
- **Composition:** widescreen cinematic scenes alternating with bold date typography and fine film-credit details; photography occupies most of the opening; content below stays legible and lightweight.
- **Opening:** a black photographic envelope with a translucent negative strip. Tap “Play our invitation”; the envelope separates and the still image expands into a short silent scene. “Open without film” is equally accessible.
- **Sections:** title frame → three story stills → premiere date → ceremonies as chapter cards → location scouting postcard → gallery as film contact sheet → RSVP ticket → end-credit thank-you.
- **Scratch:** remove a silver ticket panel to reveal a private joke or “Your seat in our story.” No gated practical information.
- **Motion:** cinematic crop changes via transform, clean cuts between chapters, deliberate 500–800 ms transitions. Music stays opt-in; video is optional progressive enhancement.
- **Asset:** editorial dusk landscape with human-scale warmth and clear text space. Real couple photos are uploaded later; demo portraits must be labelled sample imagery.

## 5. The Sunday Edit — made by hand, made for you

- **World:** layered cut paper, butter yellow, cobalt, tomato-red, cream; scrapbook cutouts and abstract flowers. Playful sophistication rather than cartoon wedding clipart.
- **Composition:** a vertical paper theatre; large sculptural title opening, generous single-column content, alternating dimensional paper pockets with small inset labels.
- **Opening:** a die-cut envelope with a crescent tab. Tap/pull the tab; the pocket drops slightly and a concertina of paper layers unfolds to form a playful scrapbook stage.
- **Sections:** names on the stage → pop-up story cards → detachable calendar square → ceremonies on folded accordion panels → illustrated route pocket → dress-code color chips → RSVP tucked into a final envelope.
- **Scratch:** scratch a tiny star-shaped foil sticker to reveal the couple's note. A single flourish rewards completion; no confetti storm.
- **Motion:** convincing hinge origins, cast shadows only on material layers, limited physical overshoot. Background and text settle quickly.
- **Asset:** tactile paper-cut scrapbook still life, visible cut edges, editorial lighting, sparse foreground so controls remain readable.

## Shared guest section system

| Observed Zareqia section | Vowvel interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Welcome, custom message, family subtext | One composed opening note; optional host/family names |
| Date, countdown, scratch, save date | Always-visible date; calendar action; optional discreet countdown; scratch reveals a keepsake note |
| Photo slideshow | Theme-specific contact sheet, tipped photograph, or gallery; no unrelated default couple photos |
| Program timeline and pre-events | One ceremony list reused for schedule, RSVP, venue, and calendar |
| Venue and Maps | Address, event-specific directions, visible timezone and pending states |
| Dress code | Short note plus accessible labeled palette chips |
| Transportation and accommodation | Compact travel guide; expandable details without a wall of text |
| Gift message | Optional host-written note, not a default cash request |
| Closing and sub-closing | One meaningful closing with optional family signature |
| RSVP | Account-free response, relevant ceremony choices, party size, confirmation/edit path |
| Translation | Language control only when actual translated content exists |
| Music | Visible opt-in control; never required to open |

Every guest page has reachable **RSVP · Directions · Save date** actions. The opening includes date and occasion, plus a skip link. Pointer scratch uses a real erase mask and completion threshold; keyboard users can reveal with one button. Reduced-motion users see the opened layout without animation. Images/video can fail without hiding content.

## Conversion website and editor

1. **Hero:** one tactile, playable envelope beside “Some invitations are opened. Yours will be remembered.” CTA: “Make it yours.” Supporting promise: free to design, pay once to publish.
2. **Five worlds:** large distinct previews; each card offers “Open invitation” and “Use this design.” Filter Wedding/Engagement preserves every entered field.
3. **Make a personal preview:** couple names inline; instant result, no account wall.
4. **The details are taken care of:** demonstrate a ceremony card, directions, calendar, and RSVP with actual interactions.
5. **Three steps:** choose your world → add your story → share your invitation.
6. **One clear purchase:** finalized total and service duration once approved; all five designs included as the proposed offer. No credits, invented discounts, or fake reviews.
7. **FAQ:** editing, photo-free designs, guest access, payment recovery, hosting, and privacy.

Editor: **Essentials → Make it yours → Ready to share.** Desktop uses controls and large preview; mobile uses full-width preview plus focused edit sheet. Auto-save on device is labeled honestly. Names, ceremony type, events, venue, welcome note, and optional story form the first working slice. Theme switching retains content. Show missing details as intentional pending wording.

Checkout demo preserves the draft and shows payment states without accepting card/UPI details. Razorpay later needs server-created orders, signature/webhook verification, idempotent fulfilment, pending recovery, and automatic publication only after confirmed payment. Future invitation addresses can be `couple.<owned-domain>`; preview must not claim an unowned domain or real public publishing.

## Build and verification order

1. Implement complete responsive homepage, five themed guest demos, one reusable content model, envelope open/skip/reset, scratch/reveal, section navigation, and functional guest utilities.
2. Add personalized editor with persistent local draft, safe reset, theme changes, and explicit demo checkout/review.
3. Produce original art assets and apply typography/layout variations; optimize image sizes; no image-baked body text.
4. Verify all themes on narrow and wide screens, keyboard-only opening/scratch, reduced motion, long names, missing photos, pending date/venue, storage recovery, and checkout back-navigation.
5. Integrate authenticated storage, actual guest replies, Razorpay, and Cloudflare publishing when credentials and commercial policy are supplied. Public demos must not claim these connected capabilities prematurely.

**Acceptance:** five unmistakably different invitation experiences; every opening bypassable; no essential content behind scratch; all demo controls respond honestly; edits survive refresh; no real charge or publication simulated as success; build passes and assets load.
