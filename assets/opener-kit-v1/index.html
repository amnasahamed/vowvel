Delicate ivory roses and foliage at the outer edges. Tactile paper, muted sage and warm cream, soft golden garden light. The envelope completely conceals the scene behind it. Tall, intimate framing; central closure clearly visible with comfortable margins. Use the supplied image only as a style and palette reference for the world hidden behind the closed object.
