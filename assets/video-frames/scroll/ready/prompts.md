# Scroll-controlled film production

Three original matched image pairs for films controlled by scroll position. These assets are distinct from the invitation-entry envelope films. Generated with built-in image generation; every last frame was edited from its inspected first frame. No competitor assets used.

Original PNGs: `assets/video-frames/scroll/`. Standardized 720 × 1280 PNG copies: `assets/video-frames/scroll/ready/`. Source images are preserved; the ready set is resized with no cropping. No movie has been generated by this asset task.

Package: `assets/video-frames/scroll/vowvel-scroll-film-frame-pairs.zip` contains six ready PNGs and this prompt set.

## Production guidance

Use the named first/last image pair as the exact video endpoints. Produce a 4–6 second silent H.264 MP4 with fast-start metadata and frequent keyframes for responsive seeking. Hold each endpoint approximately 0.3 seconds; use steady, continuous movement with no cuts. Camera and background props stay fixed. Do not bake names, dates, photos, buttons or other editable content into the film. Live HTML supplies accessible text over or beside the scene. Keep a static final-frame fallback for reduced motion, slow connections or unavailable video. Actual integration paths and scroll behavior are supplied by the app implementation; these files alone do not enable video playback.
