# Vowvel logo v2 — The Fold

Generated with built-in image generation. Raster concept; production vector refinement remains.

## Direction

Make the name the primary identity. The V and v use a folded-paper incision, connecting the lettering with invitations. Rounded counters add warmth; aubergine ink on ivory supports the luxury direction.

## Comparisons and ideation

Design observations: CELINE demonstrates typographic restraint; Cartier demonstrates distinctive signature lettering. LOEWE documents its four-L motif's origins in leather branding irons, illustrating how craft can inform a symbol.

Directions considered: handwritten signature; ceremonial seal; folded-paper wordmark. Selected the folded-paper wordmark for its connection to invitations and a clearer website header silhouette.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create a highly resolved original luxury wordmark for Vowvel, a contemporary wedding and engagement invitation platform. Concept: THE FOLD — the tactile beauty of a folded invitation translated into letterforms. This should look like an exceptional independent type designer's identity for an intimate contemporary luxury house.

Exact and only text: "Vowvel" spelled V-o-w-v-e-l. Wordmark is the hero, large and perfectly optically spaced, centered on a flat warm porcelain ivory field. Deep aubergine-black ink. Draw bespoke sculptural serif lettering with confident medium-weight stems, gently flared chiseled serifs, generous open counters, and refined moderate stroke contrast. Wide elegant rounded o and e, graceful angular w and v, tall refined final l. The uppercase V has an original subtly folded upper right terminal, a tiny precise negative-space incision that suggests paper folding. Echo this fold detail very subtly in the lowercase v. Keep all six letters immediately legible. Warm, sensual, assured and editorial. The word should feel drawn as a whole, with beautifully balanced rhythm; not typed in a stock Didot font. No sprawling swashes, no hairline fragility.

One single finished wordmark only, no separate oversized monogram, no additional icon. Wordmark fills approximately 65% of the landscape canvas width. Ample immaculate negative space. Flat vector-like logo artwork with exceptionally crisp edges. No board, no presentation labels, no tagline, no mockup, no 3D, no foil, no gradients, no shadows, no textures, no hearts, no rings, no flowers, no crowns. Do not reproduce any existing luxury house lettering. Render an original premium identity with one memorable typographic idea, restraint, and exacting craft.

## Presentation edit prompt

Keep the exact Vowvel wordmark design, proportions, six letters, placement and aubergine ink unchanged. Change only the background: place the logo on a completely opaque solid warm ivory background #F7F3EB. Absolutely no transparency; every background pixel must be opaque ivory. Clean flat logo presentation, no textures, shadows, gradients or added text.
