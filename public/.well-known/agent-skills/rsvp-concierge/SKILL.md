# Skill: Vowvel RSVP Concierge

Collect, validate, and manage wedding and celebration RSVP responses for invited guests.

## Usage

Use this skill when processing guest replies, dietary preferences, ceremony attendance, and party sizes.

## Actions

- **Submit RSVP**: Send guest attendance choice (`attending` or `declining`), party size (1–10), specific ceremony selections (e.g. Haldi, Sangeet, Ceremony, Reception), contact phone/email, and notes to `/api/rsvp/:slug`.
- **Query Status**: Check existing attendance records and headcount summaries.
